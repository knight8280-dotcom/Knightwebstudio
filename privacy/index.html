Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it

Short version: we only keep what you send us through the enquiry form or at checkout, we use it to reply to you and run your project, and we don't sell or share it.

Last updated 2 October 2026

The enquiry form

When you send the form on the contact page, we receive what you entered: your name, email address, business name, the package you're interested in, the services you ticked and your message. It is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-relay service that passes the submission on and does not keep a copy for us to read later.

We use it for one thing: replying to you about your project. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell, rent or share your details with anyone else. If you'd like your enquiry deleted, email us and we'll remove it.

Spam protection

The contact page loads hCaptcha to tell people from bots. hCaptcha may set a cookie and collect device and browser information for that purpose, under its own privacy policy. It loads only on the contact page, and only once you reach the form.

Stored on your device

The site keeps two small things in your browser's local storage, never on our servers:

  • Your theme choice (light or dark), so it stays the way you set it.
  • A draft of the enquiry form, saved as you type, so a half-written message survives an accidental tab close. It is cleared when the form sends.

Nothing in local storage is transmitted anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Analytics and cookies

We use Plausible Analytics to count visits, see which pages people read, and know when an enquiry is sent or a call is booked. It sets no cookies, stores no personal data and does not follow you across other sites. There is no advertising pixel and no tracking cookie on this site.

Booking a call

The "Book a call" buttons open Calendly, a scheduling service, in a new tab. If you book, Calendly receives the name, email and any notes you enter, under its own privacy policy, and it may set its own cookies on its page. We use those details only to hold the call.

Payments

If you pay a deposit or start a care plan on our checkout page, the payment is handled by Stripe. You enter your card or bank details, name, email and billing address into Stripe's secure form, under Stripe's own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card or bank account number. Stripe tells us your name, email, what you paid for and the amount, and sends you a receipt.

We use those details to confirm your payment and run your project. Our confirmation email is sent through Resend, an email delivery service. Paying doesn't add you to a mailing list.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors for security and operational reasons under its own privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs. The pricing and home pages also contact our payment server, hosted by Render, so checkout is ready when you click; Render may log the IP address of that request in the same way.

Links to other sites

Our work pages link to client sites we built. Each of those has its own privacy practices, and this page doesn't cover them.
