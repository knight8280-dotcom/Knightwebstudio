FAQ

Questions we get asked

The things people ask before they hire us.

How long does a website take?

An information website is usually live in 7–10 days. A full business site runs 2–4 weeks, and stores take 4–6. The biggest variable is how quickly content and photos come back to us — we'll tell you exactly what we need on day one.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo if you have one, any photos you want used, and a rough idea of the pages you need. If you don't have copy written, that's fine. On Business and Commerce we draft it and you edit; on Starter we'll polish what you send. If you don't have a logo, we can design one.

Do I own the website?

Completely. The domain is registered in your name, the code and design are yours on final payment, and you can move to another provider at any time. We don't hold anything hostage.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. Sites that need frequent changes are built on a CMS so you can edit text, images and blog posts without touching code. You get a recorded walkthrough of your own site at handover.

Do I have to take a care plan?

No — you can take the site and host it wherever you like. Most clients stay on a plan because hosting, security updates, backups, monitoring and monthly content edits bundled for $300 a month on the Essential plan works out cheaper than paying for those separately and calling someone hourly when something breaks. There are three tiers so a brochure site isn't subsidising a busy store. Plans are month-to-month and you can stop any time.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?

For some businesses, you should. If you need a simple page, have the time to keep it current, and don't rely on search to bring in customers, a website builder is good value. Where builders tend to fall short is page speed, how well you show up in local search, and the small details that turn a visitor into an enquiry. And when something breaks, there's no one who knows your site. We build for businesses where the website has to bring in work. If a builder is the better fit for you, we'll say so on the call.

What about hosting and the domain?

We can set both up and bill them through a care plan, or point everything at accounts you own and hand you the keys. Either way we handle the technical setup, SSL and email records.

What if I already have a website?

We rebuild existing sites all the time. Content gets migrated, old URLs get redirected to the new ones so you keep your search rankings, and the switch-over happens with no downtime.

How does payment work?

The standard option is 50% to book the project and start design, 50% on launch. Payment plans are available too: 50% up front, then the rest in up to 3 monthly payments. Everything is fixed-price from the proposal — no hourly surprises.

Thirty minutes, no pitch. You'll get a clear view of what your site needs and what it should cost — even if you build it yourself.
