Why automate

The hours that disappear every week

Surveys of small business owners have found admin taking 11 to 16 hours of their week. Most of it is the same handful of small jobs. One at a time they seem small. Added up, they cost you a working week every couple of months.

An example week for a small service business
Done by handExampleShare of the weekTime a week
Returning missed calls and voicemails5 calls × 6 minutes30 min
Booking appointments by phone and text10 bookings × 8 minutes80 min

Questions

Before you start

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not much. We start with what you already use, like your phone, calendar, email and Google profile, and only add a tool where it saves real time. Where a service is needed, such as a business texting line or a booking tool, it runs on an account in your name and its fee is paid straight to the provider. We pick the lowest-cost option that does the job and tell you the monthly cost before you decide.

Will my customers feel like they're talking to a robot?

No. The messages are short, friendly and written in your voice, and they come from your business number or email. The automation handles the first reply and the reminders; you still have the real conversation.

Do I need a new website first?

No. Most setups work with the website you have, or with just your Google profile and phone number. If your site is costing you customers, we'll say so in the audit and give you the fixed price to fix it.

Is the revenue estimate a guarantee?

No. The calculator is a way to see what the numbers could look like for your business, using figures you enter. Results depend on your market, your prices and how quickly you answer the leads. What we do guarantee is the fixed price and the work listed for it.
