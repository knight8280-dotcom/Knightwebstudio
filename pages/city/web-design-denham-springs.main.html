How long will my site take?

An information website can be live in as little as 7–10 days. A full business site runs 2–4 weeks, and stores take 4–6. The biggest variable is how quickly content and photos come back to us.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Sites that need frequent changes are built on a CMS, so you can edit text, images and posts without touching code, and you get a recorded walkthrough of your own site at handover.
