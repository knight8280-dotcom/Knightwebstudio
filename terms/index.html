This page sums up, in plain language, how a project with KnightWebstudio runs. Before any work starts, you sign a project agreement for your project, and that signed agreement controls the details.

Standard payment terms

Every price is fixed and confirmed in writing before work begins. The standard terms are a 50% deposit to book your project and start work, and the other 50% at launch.

Payment plan

If you'd rather spread the cost, pay the 50% deposit to start, then the rest in up to 3 equal monthly payments. The first monthly payment is due one month after the deposit, so everything is paid within 4 months at most.

On Starter, for example, that's $1,000 down, then about $333 a month for three months.

Invoices and how to pay

Invoices are due within 7 days. You can pay by card or ACH bank transfer through Stripe, our payment processor, or by check on request.

Late payments

If an invoice goes unpaid, we'll send you a written reminder first. If it's still unpaid 10 days after that reminder, interest of 1% per month is added to the overdue amount until it's paid.

Revision rounds

Each package includes a set number of rounds of changes:

  • One-page: 1 revision round.
  • Starter: 1 revision round.
  • Business: up to 3 design rounds, plus a final round of tweaks on the finished site before launch.
  • Commerce: up to 3 design rounds, plus a final round of tweaks on the finished site before launch.

Your site's text

On One-page and Starter, you supply the text for your site, and we edit and polish it. On Business and Commerce, we draft the copy and you edit it.

Care plans

Care plans ($125, $300, $600 or $1,000 a month) are billed monthly. To cancel or change your plan, give us 10 days' written notice (email is fine). There's no fee, and the notice gives us time to transfer your accounts and walk you through the handover. Your plan keeps running through the end of the month you've already paid for, and there are no partial refunds. If your written notice arrives less than 10 days before the next billing date, that next month is still billed and service runs through it.

Consulting and automation

The Growth Audit ($350) and strategy sessions ($150) are paid when booked. If you book a project within 30 days of your Growth Audit, the $350 comes off that project's price. Automation setups ($500, $750 or $1,500) are 50% to start and 50% when they go live, and include a recorded walkthrough and 30 days of fixes. Software, phone and texting services run on accounts in your name, and their fees are paid by you directly to the provider. Advisor ($500 a month) and Partner ($1,000 a month) plans are billed monthly and cancel the same way as care plans: 10 days' written notice, no fee. On the Partner plan, unused hands-on hours don't roll over to the next month.

The time and revenue figures on our site, including the calculator, are estimates to help you plan. They are not a promise or a guarantee of results.

Your project agreement

This page is a summary, not the full contract. The project agreement you sign controls the details, including cancellation fees. If anything here differs from your signed agreement, the agreement applies.
