Questions

Baton Rouge questions, answered

Do you only work with Baton Rouge businesses?

No, we work with clients anywhere. But Baton Rouge is home, so local clients can meet us in person, and we know the area your customers are searching from.

How much does a website cost?

Our prices are fixed and published: Starter $2,000, Business $4,000, Commerce $8,000. Care plans are $300, $600 or $1,000 a month. You pay 50% to book and 50% at launch as standard, or take a payment plan: 50% up front, then the rest in up to 3 equal monthly payments.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes, we rebuild existing sites all the time. Content gets migrated, old URLs are redirected to the new ones so you keep your search rankings, and the switch-over happens with no downtime.
