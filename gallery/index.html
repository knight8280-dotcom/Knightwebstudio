.faq

FAQ list

Do I own the website?

Completely. The domain is registered in your name, the code and design are yours on final payment, and you can move to another provider at any time. We don't hold anything hostage.

How long does a website take?

A one-page site can be live in as little as 7 days, and an information website in as little as 7–10 days. A full business site runs 2–4 weeks, and stores take 4–6. The biggest variable is how quickly content and photos come back to us — we'll tell you exactly what we need on day one.
