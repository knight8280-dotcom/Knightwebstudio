How automation works

What happens when a customer calls and you can't pick up

Follow one call from the missed ring to the review. Each step runs on its own, on accounts in your name.

  1. 1

    You can't pick up.

    You're on a job. Normally that means a voicemail, a round of phone tag, or a caller who tries the next company on Google.

  2. 2

    Missed-call text-back · $750 setup

    Within a minute, they get a text.

    The caller gets a text from your business number asking what they need. Their reply lands on your phone, so you call back knowing the job.
