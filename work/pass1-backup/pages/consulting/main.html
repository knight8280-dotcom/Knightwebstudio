Why automate

The hours that disappear every week

Surveys of small business owners have found admin taking 11 to 16 hours of their week. Most of it is the same handful of small jobs: calling people back, booking appointments, chasing quotes, copying details from one place to another and remembering to ask for reviews. One at a time they seem small. Added up, they cost you a working week every couple of months.

How it works

What happens, step by step

No new habits to learn. Each automation watches for one thing, like a missed call, a new enquiry or a finished job, and does the next step for you. It all runs on accounts in your name.

Missed-call text-back $750

  1. You're on a job and can't pick up.
  2. Within a minute, the caller gets a text from your business number: "Sorry we missed your call, this is [your business]. What can we help with?"
  3. They reply by text, and the conversation lands on your phone with their number saved.
  4. You call back when you're free, already knowing what they need.

Saves: voicemail checks and phone tag, and the callers who would otherwise try the next company on Google.

Online booking & reminders $750

  1. A customer picks a time on your website or Google profile, day or night.
  2. It goes straight onto your calendar, with no double bookings.
  3. They get a confirmation right away and a reminder the day before.
  4. Need to move it? They reschedule from the reminder, and your calendar updates itself.

Saves: the back-and-forth of booking by phone, and the no-shows from forgotten appointments.

Lead follow-up system $1,500

  1. An enquiry comes in from your website, Google, Facebook or a missed call.
  2. It's added to one list automatically, with the date and what they asked for.
  3. If they haven't booked after 2 days, a friendly follow-up goes out, and another after a week.
  4. When the job is done, they get a review request with a direct link to your Google page.

Saves: copying details by hand, and the quotes that go cold because nobody followed up.

Custom automation $1,500 per workflow

  1. We map one process that eats your time: quote emails, intake forms, invoices or the questions customers ask every day.
  2. We connect the tools you already use, so the information moves on its own instead of being retyped.
  3. Where it helps, the reply, the quote or the paperwork is drafted for you, and you approve it before it goes out.
  4. You get a recorded walkthrough, and we fix anything that breaks in the first 30 days.

Saves: the evenings spent at the desk on email and paperwork.

Questions

Before you start

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not much. We start with what you already use, like your phone, calendar, email and Google profile, and only add a tool where it saves real time. Where a service is needed, such as a business texting line or a booking tool, it runs on an account in your name and its fee is paid straight to the provider. We pick the lowest-cost option that does the job and tell you the monthly cost before you decide.

Will my customers feel like they're talking to a robot?

No. The messages are short, friendly and written in your voice, and they come from your business number or email. The automation handles the first reply and the reminders; you still have the real conversation.

Do I need a new website first?

No. Most setups work with the website you have, or with just your Google profile and phone number. If your site is costing you customers, we'll say so in the audit and give you the fixed price to fix it.

Is the revenue estimate a guarantee?

No. The calculator is a way to see what the numbers could look like for your business, using figures you enter. Results depend on your market, your prices and how quickly you answer the leads. What we do guarantee is the fixed price and the work listed for it.
